Figure 1. mFLOW-Seq workflow overview. The schematic shows matched FRT sampling, microbial enrichment, IgA-PE/IgM-APC/IgG-FITC labelling, operational DAPI gating and sequencing. DAPI was added at room temperature immediately before sorting, within approximately five minutes, without a subsequent wash. SYTO9/PI pilot aliquots were separate; same-event dye concordance has not been established from the recovered records. The five illustrated sorting targets are IgA-positive, IgM-positive, IgG-positive, IgA/IgM double-positive and antibody-unbound populations. PRE was collected before staining and is not a sorted fraction. The retained sequencing export contains 149 PRE/IgA/IgM/IgG libraries from 15 women, whereas the clinical records identify 16 women. Arrows show the general workflow, not an endpoint inclusion flow or evidence that every sorting target entered the retained export. The inset is illustrative and does not reconstruct original gates, percentage denominators, purity or viability. Illustrations were generated using BioRender.
