Figure 2. PRE communities differ among the observed woman/site profiles. (A) Relative assigned abundance within the 39-assignment baseline, aggregated to recorded genus. Six genera with the largest total abundance across 36 equally weighted observed woman/site profiles are displayed; the remainder are pooled. Only observed woman/site profiles are displayed, grouped by site and labelled by participant. Unavailable PRE observations are omitted from the display, without zero-filling or imputation. (B) Shannon entropy was calculated per preparation and then averaged within woman/site; lines connect available sites from the same woman. Participant 99’s two endometrial preparations contribute one averaged composition and one mean preparation Shannon value. The plot contains 37 libraries from 15 women: vagina 15/15 libraries/women, cervix 8/8 and endometrium 14/13. It is descriptive and does not validate taxonomic accuracy, contamination removal, viability or residence.
