Supplementary Figure S6. Observed PRE assignments accumulate across women. Lines show the exact expected number of observed assignments among equally likely subsets of k women, separately by site and historical taxon membership (39 solid; 41 dashed). Available PRE data comprise 15 vaginal libraries from 15 women, 8 cervical libraries from 8 women, and 14 endometrial libraries from 13 women. Participant 99 contributes one endometrial profile after averaging two normalized preparations; presence therefore includes an assignment detected in either preparation. These curves describe accumulation within the filtered, observed dataset. They have no confidence bands and do not establish read-depth adequacy, unseen richness or complete community coverage. Overlapping curves indicate identical expected values for the two memberships at that site.
