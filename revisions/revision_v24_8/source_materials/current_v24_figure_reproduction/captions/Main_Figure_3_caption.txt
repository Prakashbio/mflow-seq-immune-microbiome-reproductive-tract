Figure 3. Reconstructed immunoglobulin-gate percentages and within-woman IgM-minus-IgA contrasts. (A–C) Each coloured point is one woman at the indicated site; black diamonds show the equal-woman mean and vertical lines its pointwise 95% percentile bootstrap interval (10,000 woman-level draws within site, seed 20261030). There are 16 vaginal, 10 cervical and 16 endometrial woman/site records. Percentages are 100 × events in the named Ig gate / events in its recorded DAPI-negative parent under P1, reconstructed from original FACSDiva PDF reports. IgA-only and IgM-only gates are separate from the IgA/IgM double-positive gate (Double +); IgG-positive events are evaluated in a separate fluorescence panel. These percentages should not be summed as a four-part partition. (D) Diamonds show the mean within-woman IgM-only minus IgA-only percentage-point difference at each site; horizontal lines show saved pointwise 95% woman-bootstrap intervals using the same draw count and seed. The dashed line marks zero difference. The main reconstruction retains the later-printed HUT090 report and HUT099’s fresh endometrial preparation. Earlier HUT090 gating and the frozen HUT099 repeat are sensitivity records. Historical gate populations do not independently establish antibody specificity, bacterial identity, viability or post-sort purity. Intervals are descriptive and pointwise; no between-site hypothesis test or significance annotation is shown. Sorting yields and sequencing-assignment counts are different endpoints and are not used to calculate these percentages.
