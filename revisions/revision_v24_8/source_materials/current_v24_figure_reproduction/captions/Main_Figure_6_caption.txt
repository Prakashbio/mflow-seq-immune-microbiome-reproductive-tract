Figure 6. Matched IgA/IgM sequencing representation and corresponding mean relative abundance by site. (A) Points show the ratio of equal-woman mean IgA abundance to the sum of IgA and IgM mean abundances for eight previously discussed provisional taxon assignments in the 39-assignment baseline. Bars are pointwise 95% woman-bootstrap intervals; point size indicates women with detection in either matched fraction, and triangles flag fewer than three detected women. The dashed line marks equal relative representation, not a threshold for per-cell binding. Vaginal, cervical and endometrial matched sets contain 15, nine and 12 women, respectively. Sparse endpoint intervals may reflect the observed detections and do not establish exclusive binding. (B) The corresponding equal-woman mean relative abundances in IgA and IgM libraries are displayed on a shared linear 0–100% colour scale. Cell labels show percentages rounded to one decimal place; 0.0 can include positive values below 0.05% and does not by itself establish absence. These are compositional sequencing measurements, not species-specific antibody-coating probabilities, affinity estimates or absolute bacterial abundances. All taxa, finite-bootstrap counts and sensitivities are supplied in Supplementary Tables S2T.1–S2T.3 and the accompanying data. Axis labels abbreviate Lactobacillus as L.
