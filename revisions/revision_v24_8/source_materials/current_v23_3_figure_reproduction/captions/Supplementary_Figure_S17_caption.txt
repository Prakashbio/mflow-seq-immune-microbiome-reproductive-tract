Supplementary Figure S17. Two recorded endometrial preparations from participant 99. Panels show Shannon entropy and Lactobacillus genus relative abundance for the fresh-labelled and frozen-labelled PRE, IgA, IgM and IgG libraries in the 39-taxon set. Dashed lines connect paired fresh and cryopreserved fragments from the same original endometrial biopsy. Cryopreserved fragments were frozen in cryoprotective medium using Mr. Frosty and then stored in liquid nitrogen, without repeated freeze–thaw cycles. Four fractions are not four independent storage replicates. There are no confidence bands, equivalence margins or TOST verdicts. Fraction-specific Bray–Curtis distances and the 41-taxon sensitivity are reported in Table S2S.3 and the statistical files. This single paired biological specimen provides a descriptive comparison and cannot estimate a general storage effect across women.
