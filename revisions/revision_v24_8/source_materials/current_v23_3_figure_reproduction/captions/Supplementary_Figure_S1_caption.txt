Supplementary Figure S1. Illustrative flow-cytometry plots. The composite labels endometrial (A), cervical (B) and vaginal (C) preparations and displays scatter, SSC-A/SSC-H, DAPI and immunoglobulin fluorescence panels. Original clinical FACSDiva PDFs additionally provide the plotted gate regions, named population hierarchy, event counts and parent percentages. In the reconstructed report tables, immunoglobulin populations are children of the DAPI-negative gate under P1; a separate named SSC-A/SSC-H parent is not shown in those statistics. Display of an SSC-A/SSC-H panel alone does not establish that identical singlet gates were applied to every clinical preparation. Table 2 is reconstructed from the report counts, not inferred from this representative composite. Exact machine-readable gate geometry, transformation/export settings, matching stained-control records and cohort-wide purity remain incompletely documented. DAPI negativity alone does not establish viability or exclude all background events.
