Supplementary Figure S14. Descriptive alpha metrics by recorded storage. Panels show Shannon entropy, Simpson diversity (1−D), richness within the 39 retained assignments, and Pielou evenness for PRE, IgA, IgM and IgG libraries by site and condition. Dots show individual libraries; boxes show medians and quartiles and whiskers extend to observed values within 1.5 interquartile ranges. Pielou evenness is undefined for six one-taxon libraries; these missing values are counted in the tables. The display includes 149 libraries from 15 women and 40 preparations; participant 99 contributes both endometrial storage preparations. No storage-effect test or equivalence inference is displayed. Exact group denominators and descriptive summaries are supplied in Tables S2S.1–S2S.2 and the accompanying files.
