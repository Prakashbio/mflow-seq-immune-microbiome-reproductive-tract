Figure 5. Community ordination and matched distance from each sorted fraction to its own PRE community. (A) Participant-connected PCoA of Bray–Curtis dissimilarities contains 149 libraries from 15 women, with panels for vagina, cervix and endometrium. Colour identifies participant and shape identifies PRE, IgA, IgM or IgG. Lines connect fractions within an anatomical preparation; they indicate matching rather than time or trajectories. Axis percentages refer to the positive eigenvalue sum after Lingoes correction for display. Tests use the uncorrected Bray–Curtis dissimilarities and their stated complete-preparation or PRE-only subsets (Table 3), not all plotted observations as independent samples. Ellipses and historical unrestricted p-values are not shown. (B) Bray–Curtis distance of each sorted fraction to its own PRE community: 104 matched distances from 37 anatomical preparations in 15 women. Colour denotes recorded menstrual-cycle phase and grey lines connect available sorted fractions within a preparation. Eight sorted libraries without a same-preparation PRE were not imputed. Within-site contrasts first average repeated-preparation differences within woman; none survives BH adjustment across the nine contrasts. Distances describe matched compositional differences and do not identify an antibody-specific effect separate from sorting. The two panels have different eligible subsets and colour keys.
