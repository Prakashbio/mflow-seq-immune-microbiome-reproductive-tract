Supplementary Figure S19A. Archived unstained and single-colour saliva controls. Columns show unstained, IgA-PE-only and IgM-APC-only preparations; rows retain all four recorded acquisition indices from 13 June 2023. Each acquisition contains 50,000 recorded events. These are 12 acquisition records, not 12 independent biological replicates. Each display bins all recorded exported events, without scatter/DAPI/Ig gating or additional compensation. Axes use arcsinh(value/150), labelled in exported channel units; colour gives events per bin on a logarithmic scale. No DAPI was used in this early series. The displays provide development-control context, not matched clinical FMO coverage or antibody-binding specificity.
