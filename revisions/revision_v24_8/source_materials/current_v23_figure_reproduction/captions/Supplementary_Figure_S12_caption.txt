Supplementary Figure S12. Across-site Shannon diversity within each woman. Points are 145 woman/site/fraction means derived from 149 libraries and 15 women in the 39-taxon baseline; colour identifies participant and panels identify fraction. Lines connect the available anatomical sites within a woman. Two endometrial preparations from participant 99 are averaged within woman/site/fraction for this display; the nested model retains both as separate preparations. Missing observations are not imputed. See Supplementary Tables S2R.1–S2R.2 for denominators and mixed-model tests. Axis abbreviations: V, vagina; C, cervix; E, endometrium.
