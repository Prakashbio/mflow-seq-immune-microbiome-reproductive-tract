Supplementary Figure S13. Descriptive alpha-diversity metrics by anatomical site and library fraction. Rows show Shannon entropy, Simpson diversity (1−D), observed richness within the 39 retained assignments, and Pielou evenness. Points represent 149 libraries from 15 women and 40 anatomical preparations; colour denotes recorded menstrual-cycle phase. Grey lines connect available fractions within a preparation. Both endometrial preparations from participant 99 remain separate preparations from the same woman. Six one-taxon libraries have undefined Pielou evenness and contribute no point in that row; their source records are retained in the accompanying data. These indices are related summaries of the same retained relative-abundance data; no separate inferential tests are presented.
