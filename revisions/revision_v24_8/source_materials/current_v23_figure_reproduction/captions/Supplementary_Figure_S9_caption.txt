Supplementary Figure S9. Descriptive sequencing profiles by recorded storage condition. (A) Lactobacillus genus relative abundance in PRE, IgA and IgM libraries, normalized over the 39 retained taxa. Dots show individual women within each site/fraction/condition; boxes show medians and quartiles, with whiskers extending to observed values within 1.5 interquartile ranges. Numbers indicate women in each displayed group. One woman contributes both endometrial storage preparations. (B) Equal-woman mean relative abundance for eight provisional assignments with the highest mean across the 18 displayed site/fraction/condition profiles; colours use a common 0–100% scale. IgG and both feature-set sensitivities are supplied in the statistical files. These are descriptive comparisons, not per-cell antibody measurements or evidence of preservation equivalence.
