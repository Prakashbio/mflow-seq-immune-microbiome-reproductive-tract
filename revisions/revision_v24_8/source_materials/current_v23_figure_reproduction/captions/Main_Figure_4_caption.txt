Figure 4. Shannon diversity with preparation matching. Each point is one of 149 libraries from 15 women and 40 anatomical preparations in the 39-taxon baseline. Grey lines connect the available PRE, IgA, IgM and IgG fractions from the same preparation; colour indicates recorded cycle phase. Both participant-99 endometrial preparations are displayed and retained in the mixed model, with participant and preparation random intercepts. No anatomical ordering or fraction significance is implied by point separation. Site/fraction counts and descriptive medians with interquartile ranges are provided in Supplementary Table S2R.1.
