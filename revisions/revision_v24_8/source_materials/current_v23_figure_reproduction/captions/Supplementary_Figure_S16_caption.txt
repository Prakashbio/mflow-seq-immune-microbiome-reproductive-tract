Supplementary Figure S16. Genus-aggregated matched IgA/IgM representation scores. Retained counts from the same 39-taxon baseline are summed within genus before library normalization. Points show equal-woman site-specific ratios and bars show pointwise 95% intervals from 5,000 woman-bootstrap draws. Point size indicates detected women; triangles flag fewer than three. The vaginal, cervical and endometrial matched sets contain 15, nine and 12 women. Aggregation is a sensitivity to taxonomic resolution, not independent validation of the classifier. Sparse/undefined bootstrap denominators are retained in the accompanying tables. No CST-I-versus-CST-III comparison is inferred.
