Figure 5. Participant-connected PCoA of Bray–Curtis dissimilarities. The descriptive ordination contains 149 libraries from 15 women, with panels for vagina, cervix and endometrium. Colour identifies participant and shape identifies PRE, IgA, IgM or IgG. Lines connect fractions within an anatomical preparation; they indicate matching rather than time or trajectories. Axis percentages refer to the positive eigenvalue sum after Lingoes correction for display. Tests use the uncorrected Bray–Curtis dissimilarities and their stated complete-preparation or PRE-only subsets (Table 3), not all plotted observations as independent samples. Ellipses and historical unrestricted p-values have been removed.
