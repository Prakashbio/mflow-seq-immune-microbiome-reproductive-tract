Supplementary Figure S13. Bray–Curtis distance of each sorted fraction to its own PRE community. The display contains 104 matched distances from 37 anatomical preparations in 15 women, with panels for site and colour for recorded cycle phase. Grey lines connect available sorted fractions within a preparation. Eight sorted libraries without a same-preparation PRE are listed separately and were not imputed. Within-site contrasts first average repeated-preparation differences within woman; none survives BH adjustment across the nine contrasts. Distances describe matched compositional differences and do not identify an antibody-specific effect separate from sorting.
