Supplementary Figure S14. Matched sorted-to-own-PRE contrast intervals. Points show the equal-woman mean difference between two sorted-fraction distances to the same PRE community, separately by site. Repeated preparation differences are averaged within woman. Bars are pointwise 95% intervals from 5,000 woman-bootstrap draws. Exact sign-flip tests assume symmetry/exchangeability of woman-level differences under the null, and BH correction covers nine site-specific contrasts; none survives correction. Pointwise interval exclusion of zero does not establish adjusted significance. Individual matched distances are shown in Supplementary Figure S13, and exact denominators and tests in Table S2R.5. This display does not validate per-cell immunoglobulin binding or establish an effect separate from sorting.
