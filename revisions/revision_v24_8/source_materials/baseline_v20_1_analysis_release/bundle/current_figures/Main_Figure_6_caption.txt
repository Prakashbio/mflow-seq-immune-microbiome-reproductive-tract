Figure 6. Matched IgA/IgM sequencing representation by site. Points show the ratio of equal-woman mean IgA abundance to the sum of IgA and IgM mean abundances for eight previously discussed provisional taxon assignments in the 39-taxon baseline. Bars are pointwise 95% woman-bootstrap intervals; point size indicates women with detection in either matched fraction, and triangles flag fewer than three detected women. The dashed line marks equal relative representation, not a threshold for per-cell binding. Vaginal, cervical and endometrial sets contain 15, nine and 12 women, respectively. Sparse endpoint intervals may reflect the observed detections and do not establish exclusive binding. All taxa, finite-bootstrap counts and sensitivities are provided in Supplementary Table S1 and the accompanying data.
