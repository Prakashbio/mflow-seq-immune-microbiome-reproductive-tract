Supplementary Figure S18B. Updated FACSDiva analysis of the historical HUT093 PBS1 buffer control. The same historical FCS acquisition was retrospectively analysed in FACSDiva; the updated report was exported on 12 November 2026. IgM-labelled region occupancy was 338/21,857 events in the DAPI-negative parent (1.5% as printed). The sample-free preparation contained DAPI but no immunoglobulin-detection antibodies or biological specimen; region names identify software gates, not antibody binding or a clinical false-positive rate. The earlier analysis (1,448/10,066; 14.39%) and its original plot are retained in the data/code archive, with both sets of counts documented after Table S2F.2. No background subtraction or change to clinical FACS results was made.
