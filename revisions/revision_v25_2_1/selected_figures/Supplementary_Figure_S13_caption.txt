Supplementary Figure S13. PRE taxon abundance by original vaginal-community description. Each point represents one woman at one anatomical site; black bars mark medians. Six previously discussed provisional assignments are shown to illustrate the exploratory comparisons, with no species-level BH-significant finding. The square-root abundance axis includes zero observations without a pseudocount. L-desc and D-desc denote the original Lactobacillus and dysbiosis composition descriptions, respectively, rather than clinical diagnoses. Vaginal group sizes are nine/six, cervical four/four and endometrial eight/five. The plot retains 99EP2; the alternative 99EP selection and every tested taxon are supplied in the statistical files. No historical pooled Shannon or unrestricted PERMANOVA annotation is retained in this display.
