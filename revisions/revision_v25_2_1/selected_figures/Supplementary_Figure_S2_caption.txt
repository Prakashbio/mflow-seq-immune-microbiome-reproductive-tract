Supplementary Figure S2. Illustrative fluorescence fields before and after sorting. SYTO™ 9 images are labelled vaginal (A–F), cervical (G–L) and endometrial (M–R). Pre-sort panels are A–C, G–I and M–O; post-sort panels are D–F, J–L and P–R. Arrows identify selected small fluorescent objects. Scale bars are labelled 10 μm; the recorded acquisition used a Zeiss Axio Observer with a 100× oil-immersion objective (NA 1.4). The pre-sort and post-sort material derives from the clinical preparations used for sequencing. The largely dried, coverslipped SYTO™ 9 preparations provide qualitative morphological evidence; they do not quantify viability, antibody specificity or sort purity. Independent participant counts and panel-level calibration are not established by these illustrations.
