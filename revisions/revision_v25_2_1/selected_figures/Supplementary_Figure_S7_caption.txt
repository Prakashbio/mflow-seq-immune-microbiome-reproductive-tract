Supplementary Figure S7. PRE profiles and retained assignment counts. The 39-assignment baseline is shown. (A) Mean preparation-level Shannon within woman and site for six women with all sites; line colors distinguish women. Participant 99’s two endometrial values are averaged. (B) Counts for 149 libraries (37 PRE, 38 IgA, 39 IgM, 35 IgG), colored by site. These are not raw-read totals or FACS events. (C) PRE Bray–Curtis distances on woman-level relative abundances: vagina–cervix, vagina–endometrium and cervix–endometrium use 8, 13 and 6 matched women, respectively. All ordered cross-woman pairs are included; distances share women. (D) Counts versus Shannon for the same libraries, colored by site. Count axes are logarithmic. Boxes show medians, quartiles and whiskers within 1.5 interquartile ranges; outliers are shown. Pair counts and plotted values are supplied as CSV. These descriptive panels do not validate sorting, QC thresholds or absence of depth confounding.
