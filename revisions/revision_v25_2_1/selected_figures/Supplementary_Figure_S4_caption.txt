Supplementary Figure S4. Descriptive reconstruction of analytical feature selection. (A) Numbers of exported assignments meeting each rule within the original-code site × fraction strata: at least 0.5% relative abundance in at least two, three or four libraries, or at least 10% in at least two libraries. Relative abundances use the sum of 268 exported assignment counts per library. An assignment passed the reconstructed primary rule if it met the 0.5%/two-library criterion in any stratum; the union reproduced the 41-taxon set. (B) Each dot is one library’s discarded-count percentage for the 268-to-41 or 41-to-39 step, using the corresponding input set as denominator; boxes show medians and quartiles, with whiskers to observed values within 1.5 interquartile ranges. The 268-to-41 step discarded 3.50% of pooled exported counts, with library percentages ranging from 0 to 79.10%. These are 149 library observations from 15 women, not independent participant replicates. Exported assignment counts are not all raw sequencing reads. These summaries neither validate contaminant status nor establish that filtering leaves community comparisons unchanged.
