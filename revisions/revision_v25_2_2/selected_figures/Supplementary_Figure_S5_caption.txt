Supplementary Figure S5. Exploratory PRE rank correlations differ across anatomical sites. Spearman correlations use one equally weighted PRE profile per woman and site: 15 women in vagina, 8 in cervix and 13 in endometrium. Two endometrial preparations from participant 99 are averaged after library normalization. The 12 displayed assignments were selected by their equal-site mean PRE relative abundance in the 39-assignment baseline; numbers refer to the shared key. Red indicates positive and blue negative rank correlation; grey denotes an undefined correlation involving a constant profile. No significance or microbial-interaction inference is made. All pairwise correlations for the 39- and 41-assignment memberships are supplied as CSV. Species-level assignments remain provisional.
