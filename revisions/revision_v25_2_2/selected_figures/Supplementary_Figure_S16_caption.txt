Supplementary Figure S16. PRE composition by recorded storage on common ordination axes. One Bray–Curtis principal coordinates analysis was calculated across all 37 PRE libraries after sample-wise normalization over the 39 retained assignments, using Lingoes correction. Panels separate anatomical sites while retaining the common axes; axis percentages use the sum of positive corrected eigenvalues. Colours identify recorded storage. The dashed line connects 99EP2 and 99EP, paired fresh and cryopreserved fragments from the same original endometrial biopsy. The line connects paired preparations and does not represent a time course. Denominators are 15 vaginal libraries/15 women, eight cervical libraries/eight women and 14 endometrial libraries/13 women. No confidence ellipse, PERMANOVA annotation or absence-of-storage-effect conclusion is shown. Library metrics, the distance matrix, correction constant, coordinates and eigenvalues are supplied.
