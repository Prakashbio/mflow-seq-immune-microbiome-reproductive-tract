Supplementary Figure S19C. Available SYTO9/PI-labelled pilot comparator acquisitions. Four saliva records from 20 June 2023 and two vaginal records from 7 July 2023 are displayed from their original exported events; each acquisition contains 20,000 events. The operator confirmed that pilot comparisons used separate aliquots of common starting material. Date and acquisition-number correspondence alone does not authenticate each file-level pair, biological n or same-event staining. All recorded events are shown as two-dimensional bin counts using arcsinh(value/150), with axes labelled in exported channel units and logarithmic colour scaling; no additional compensation or bacterial, live/dead or sorting gate is reconstructed. These displays do not supply a validated comparator denominator, concordance statistic or independent validation sample size.
