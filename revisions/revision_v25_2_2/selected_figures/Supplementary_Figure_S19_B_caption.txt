Supplementary Figure S19B. Original operator-drawn DAPI pilot regions. The first numbered saliva preparation from 20 June 2023 and first numbered vaginal preparation from 7 July 2023 are shown using cropped original graph bitmaps, without changed gate positions, event colours or signal adjustment. They contain 50,000 and 20,000 recorded events, respectively. DAPI roles follow the operator’s description. The complete records contain four saliva and two vaginal DAPI/antibody acquisitions; acquisition indices do not establish independent biological n. These regions are not a validated live/dead partition or a quantitative comparison with SYTO9/PI. Full source graphics are retained in the reproduction files.
