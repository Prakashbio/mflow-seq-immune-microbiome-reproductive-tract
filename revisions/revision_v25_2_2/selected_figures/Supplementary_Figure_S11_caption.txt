Supplementary Figure S11. Paired Lactobacillus relative library abundances. Panels show four provisional Lactobacillus assignments across the three sites. Lines connect the mean IgA and IgM library abundance of the same woman/site, with repeated matched preparations averaged within fraction. Abundance is expressed as a percentage of retained library counts. These paired values underlie the representation scores and are not measurements of antibody density, the proportion of cells coated, or community-level CST-specific immune activity. No anatomical decline, per-species binding exclusivity or health-protective mechanism is inferred from the plot.
